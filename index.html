Frequently Asked Questions

Simple answers to everyday questions about online safety.

How do I know if a link sent to me in a text message is fake? +
Fake text links usually urge you to act fast (e.g., "Your package is delayed" or "Your bank account is locked"). You can copy the web address from the text message and paste it into ShieldSense to check if it's safe before clicking.
Is ShieldSense free to use? +
Yes! You can check links and emails here for free anytime to make sure you stay safe while browsing online.
What should I do if I already clicked a suspicious link? +
If you entered a password on a fake site, change that password immediately on the real company's official website. If you shared credit card info, call your bank right away to block any fraud.
Does ShieldSense save or store my private messages? +
No. Whatever link or text message snippet you paste into ShieldSense is checked immediately for threats and is never shared or stored on our servers.
